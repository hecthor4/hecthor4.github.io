About me

I started programming around 2019 in Scratch. My journey there ended in 2021, when I became a micro-celebrity with a lot of popular games. I believe I have inspired a lot of children into the development world.

After that, I started programming in Python, where I didn't make anything noteworthy.

Years later, in early 2025, I got a new computer powerful enough to run the Unity Engine. I began creating videogames under my own studio ("Escape from Development"). The studio consists of two close friends and me as programmer.

I have participated in 5 game jams. Them being:
- Jam Weekend #8 - with my game "Block Burst" (ranking: 7/10)
- Wierd Game Jam III - with "The other side of the moon" (ranking: 2/10)
- Literally Anything Game Jam 3 - with "Please mom don't kick me out" (ranking: 18/31)
- Winter Game Jam 2025 - with "Crotolamator" (8/67)
- New Wierd Game Jam - with "Last Night's Dream" (1/72)

New Wierd Game Jam (it's actually spelled right) didn't have just one winner, but a winner per category. There isn't another game that surpasses mine in every category simultaniously, making me the general winner.
